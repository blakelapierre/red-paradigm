# Mars Paradigm – Faction Concepts & Modifiers
Six core factions. Each has personality, preferred strategies, unique unit component or building, and numeric modifiers. All can pursue any victory; modifiers make certain paths more natural.

| Faction | Ideology / Focus | Key Modifiers | Unique Element | Preferred Paths |
|---------|------------------|---------------|----------------|-----------------|
| **Ares Industrial** | Rapid scaling, heavy extraction | +20% mineral/energy yield, +15% construction speed, +10% ecological disruption | Heavy Excavator chassis (higher throughput, more dust) | Economic, Military, fast Transcendence via industry |
| **Gaia Stewardship** | Careful ecology, long-term stability | +25% biological/soil terraforming speed, –20% ecological disruption, slower early industry | Pioneer Microbe special (better succession) | Peace, ecological Transcendence, long games |
| **Helios Resource** | Markets, volatiles, propellant | +15% trade efficiency, better Sabatier yields, economic victory progress bonus | Resource Crawler unit (mobile processing) | Economic dominance, flexible mid-game |
| **Prometheus Collective** | Science, sensors, advanced tech | +20% research, +15% probe/sensor success, slightly higher unit costs | Advanced Sensor package | Tech racing, intel, any late victory |
| **Vanguard Compact** | Security, territorial control | +15% unit strength/defense, faster military production, moderate industry | Hardened Rover chassis | Military, defensive economic, denial of rival projects |
| **Autonomist Path** | Automation, self-replication, outward focus | +20% automation efficiency, cheaper late industry, transcendence project bonus | Self-Replicating Former special (late) | Transcendence, high-automation peace or economic |

**Diplomatic flavor:**  
Industrial and Vanguard lean competitive. Gaia and Autonomist more open to cooperation if stability is high. Helios transactional. Prometheus opportunistic.

**Starting differences:** Small variations in initial power type, location preferences, or starter unit. No faction is locked out of core systems.

These create the asymmetric multiplayer matchups that made Alpha Centauri LAN sessions memorable while staying grounded in Martian priorities.
