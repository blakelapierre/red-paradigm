# Mars Paradigm – Detailed Tech Tree (Sample)
**Trunks are parallel and cross-linked. Numbers indicate approximate tier (1 = early, 5 = late). Educational notes in italics.**

## ISRU & Materials Trunk
- **Atmospheric Processing (1)**  
  Unlocks: Basic CO₂ capture and solid-oxide electrolysis (O₂ production).  
  *MOXIE-style: extract oxygen from the 95% CO₂ atmosphere. Power-intensive but immediately useful for life support and oxidizer.*

- **Water Extraction (1)** (requires Atmospheric Processing or starting tech)  
  Unlocks: Ice mining / Rodwell systems, basic water purification.  
  *Subsurface ice is the primary hydrogen and water source. Geographic placement matters.*

- **Sabatier Synthesis (2)** (requires Water Extraction + Atmospheric Processing)  
  Unlocks: Methane + oxygen propellant production, closed-loop hydrogen recycling.  
  *Classic ISRU propellant path. Enables surface-to-orbit capability and energy storage.*

- **Regolith Refining (2)**  
  Unlocks: Metal extraction, silicon processing, basic sintering/construction feedstock.  
  *Martian soil contains iron, aluminum, silicon. Perchlorates must be managed.*

- **Advanced Manufacturing (3)** (requires Regolith Refining)  
  Unlocks: Additive manufacturing at scale, structural components, spare parts independence.

- **Biomanufacturing (3)** (cross-link Biology)  
  Unlocks: Microbial production of bioplastics, organics from local feedstocks.  
  *Reduces reliance on imported polymers; useful for habitats and seals.*

- **High-Throughput Industry (4–5)**  
  Unlocks: Self-expanding industrial capacity, large-scale material output for late projects and transcendence.

## Energy Systems Trunk
- **Optimized Solar (1)**  
  Unlocks: High-efficiency panels, dust mitigation.  
  *Mars insolation is ~43% of Earth’s; dust is a constant issue.*

- **Compact Nuclear (2)**  
  Unlocks: Fission reactors for reliable baseload. Critical for polar or high-latitude sites and continuous ISRU.

- **Advanced Storage & Distribution (3)**  
  Unlocks: Better batteries, thermal storage, local grids.

- **Beamed / Orbital Concepts (4)** (late)  
  Unlocks: Experimental power beaming or large orbital collectors (high cost, high reward).

## Climate & Atmosphere Trunk
- **Local Warming Techniques (1)**  
  Unlocks: Aerogel / solid-state greenhouses, basic mirrors, passive solar concentrators.  
  *Creates habitable micro-environments without global change.*

- **Aerosol Engineering (2–3)**  
  Unlocks: Manufactured nanorods / engineered particles for regional greenhouse effect.  
  *Extremely mass-efficient warming method using local iron/aluminum. Requires continuous replenishment.*

- **Regional Atmospheric Processors (3)**  
  Unlocks: Larger-scale gas release, pressure contribution, condenser-style water recovery.

- **Retention & Shielding Concepts (4–5)**  
  Unlocks: Experimental magnetic or electrostatic ideas, advanced atmosphere management.  
  *Full open breathable atmosphere remains extremely distant; these improve regional viability and reduce losses.*

## Biology & Soil Trunk
- **Perchlorate Remediation (1–2)**  
  Unlocks: Chemical or biological soil washing. Essential before open or semi-open agriculture.

- **Engineered Pioneer Microbes (2–3)**  
  Unlocks: Extremophile strains tolerant of low pressure, radiation, cold, and residual toxins. Begin slow oxygen and organic production.

- **Pioneer Plants & Succession (3–4)**  
  Unlocks: Hardy plants, soil building, increased biological oxygen contribution.  
  *Oxygen timescales are centuries even under optimistic rates; still valuable for long games and ecological stability.*

- **Ecological Maturation (5)**  
  Unlocks: Complex local ecosystems, significant regional habitability bonuses, peace-victory support.

## Propulsion & Logistics Trunk
- **Surface Mobility (1)**  
  Unlocks: Efficient rovers, basic hoppers.

- **Propellant Logistics (2)** (cross Sabatier)  
  Unlocks: Local refueling, extended range.

- **Interplanetary Capability (4–5)**  
  Unlocks: Reliable cargo and seed-ship systems. Directly feeds Transcendence victory.

## Sensors, Intel & Automation Trunk
- **Basic Probes & Sensors (1)**  
  Unlocks: Scout units, limited fog reveal.

- **Sensor Webs (2–3)**  
  Unlocks: Persistent monitoring, project-progress detection.

- **Advanced Infiltration (3–4)**  
  Unlocks: Higher-success probe actions, sabotage options.

- **Automation & AI Assistance (4)**  
  Unlocks: Reduced micromanagement, smarter formers, late-game production bonuses.

## Social / Governance (Parallel Track)
Choices affect efficiency, ecological impact, diplomacy, and unit behavior (inspired by AC social engineering but lighter):
- Industrial Focus vs Ecological Restraint
- Centralized vs Distributed
- Open Research vs Proprietary
Each combination modifies production, terraforming speed, intel success, or diplomatic modifiers.

**Design notes:**  
Techs primarily unlock *capabilities and components* rather than pure percentage bonuses. Many have clear educational tooltips. Cross-links encourage mixed strategies. Early techs are cheap; late techs require significant research investment and industrial base.
