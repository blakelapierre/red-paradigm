# Red Paradigm – Arrival & Start Condition Notes
**Naming:** Primary term is “the Red.” Sparse in-fiction uses of “Mars” (historical references, certain faction dialogue) are acceptable; it is not the default or title term.

## Simultaneous Player Start + Historical Stagger
- All players begin on the exact same turn.
- That turn is defined as the operational arrival of the final faction.
- Factions that “arrived earlier” (in the fiction) already have progress on the map when play begins.
- Progress level is determined by an arrival offset assigned at scenario setup.

## What Earlier Factions Can Have
- Additional bases or expanded primary base
- Active power and ISRU processors
- Local terraforming (greenhouses, soil work, small warming)
- Extra formers, rovers, or stored volatiles/propellant
- Minor tech or project head-start
- Sensor coverage or claimed resource nodes

## What No One Has at Start
- Global climate transformation
- Completed major Paradigm projects (these remain raceable)
- Overwhelming military dominance (keeps early game contested)

## Player Experience
You drop in with your faction’s starter package at the same moment everyone else does. Some rivals already have lights on, processors running, and a few improved tiles. The race is to catch up, leapfrog via projects and tech, cooperate, or contest what they built—while the planet’s real constraints still apply to everyone.

## Narrative Hooks
Opening interlude can show earlier landings as brief flashes, then settle on the final touchdown. In-game text refers to “the first landings,” “those already established,” or “the Red as we found it.”

This change is purely structural and narrative; all mechanical systems (ISRU loops, terraforming stages, unit design, projects, victories) stay intact.
