# Mars Paradigm – Unit Designer Rules & Component List
Modular designer similar to Alpha Centauri. Player (or faction) combines components unlocked by tech. Cost scales with power and specials.

## Chassis (Base Mobility & Role)
- **Personnel / Infantry** – Flexible, can garrison, basic probe capable.
- **Rover** – Primary surface workhorse. Good cargo and former mount.
- **Flyer / Drone** – Fast recon, light payload, sensor focused.
- **Hopper** – Short ballistic hops (requires propellant tech). Extended range.
- **Stationary Platform** – High-power processors, sensors, or defenses. No movement.
- **Former / Construction** – Dedicated terraforming and building unit (can be rover- or platform-based).

## Power Sources
- Solar (cheap, dust-vulnerable)
- Battery / Stored
- Nuclear (reliable, heavier)
- Methane-Oxygen (mobility bonus once Sabatier online)

## Primary Function (choose one main)
- **Terraformer Toolset** – Performs ISRU and terraforming tasks (speed modified by project and tech).
- **Processor** – Mobile or stationary resource conversion.
- **Sensor / Probe** – Reveals fog, detects projects, can attempt infiltration.
- **Weapon** – Kinetic, energy, or area (dust/disruption). Strength scales with tech.
- **Cargo / Logistics** – Hauls resources or components between sites.
- **Habitat / Support** – Temporary life support or construction aid.

## Specials (0–3 typically)
- Radiation Hardened
- Low-Power Mode
- Ecological (reduced disruption, biology bonus)
- Dust Resistant
- Self-Repair
- High-Throughput (former or processor)
- Probe Defense
- Faction-unique (see faction doc)

## Design Rules
- Chassis determines base move, cargo, and which functions are efficient.
- Function + Power + Specials determine final stats and abilities.
- Tech unlocks new components; Paradigm projects can grant temporary or unique access.
- Cost in minerals + energy (or propellant for some).
- Designer UI shows clear trade-offs and educational notes (e.g., “Nuclear power enables continuous polar operation but increases mass”).

This preserves the custom-unit fun while mapping directly onto realistic Martian roles (formers, ISRU plants, scouts, defenders, logistics).
