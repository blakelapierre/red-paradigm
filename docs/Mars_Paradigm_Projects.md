# Mars Paradigm – Unique Projects (“Paradigms”)
Raceable, planet-unique projects. Only one of each can exist. Partial intel can reveal that a rival is building one. Capture of the hosting base transfers control. Costs are relative (minerals/energy/research investment).

| Project | Approx. Tier | Key Prerequisite | Primary Effects | Educational / Flavor Note |
|---------|--------------|------------------|-----------------|---------------------------|
| **Atmospheric Accelerator** (Weather Paradigm analog) | Early-Mid | Local Warming + Atmospheric Processing | +50% terraforming/former speed (most tasks). Unlocks advanced regional tools (better aerosols, condensers, rapid soil work) even before normal tech. | Directly amplifies the core terraforming loop. Early race creates lasting map and economic advantage. |
| **Planetary Resource Exchange** | Early-Mid | Sabatier + basic trade tech | Enables formal resource markets. Owner gains economic leverage, better trade rates, and progress toward economic victory. | Turns volatiles, propellant, and energy into strategic commodities. |
| **Biological Foundry** | Mid | Engineered Microbes | Greatly accelerates biological succession and oxygen/organic contribution in owner’s regions. Supports ecological and peace paths. | Shows the slow but compounding power of biology once conditions allow. |
| **Sensor Web Array** | Mid | Sensor Webs | Wide persistent intel. Reveals more enemy project progress and resource nodes. Probe defense bonus. | Incomplete information remains, but owner sees further. |
| **High-Efficiency ISRU Hub** | Mid | Advanced Manufacturing | Major boost to all ISRU yields in a large radius. Reduces power cost of key processes. | Embodies the industrial throughput reality of planetary engineering. |
| **Regional Climate Stabilizer** | Mid-Late | Aerosol Engineering + Regional Processors | Stronger, more persistent regional warming and pressure effects. Reduces dust-storm disruption. | Highlights maintenance requirements of engineered climate. |
| **Industrial Seed Complex** | Late | High-Throughput Industry | Enables rapid self-expanding manufacturing. Major transcendence progress. Production boom. | The point where Mars industry can support outward expansion. |
| **Radiation Mitigation Array** | Mid-Late | Advanced materials + energy | Significant radiation reduction in covered or regional areas. Enables more open habitats and biological work. | Radiation is a constant constraint; mitigation is local/regional first. |
| **Closed-Loop Biosphere Network** | Late | Ecological Maturation | Large stability and habitability bonuses. Strongly supports Global Peace victory. Shared benefits if diplomatic conditions met. | Rewards long-term ecological investment. |
| **Interplanetary Logistics Spine** | Late | Interplanetary Capability | Unlocks reliable seed-ship and cargo capability. Direct Transcendence enabler. | Mars becomes a true springboard rather than the final destination. |
| **Autonomous Former Swarm** | Mid-Late | Automation | Formers operate with far less oversight; higher terraforming throughput. | Quality-of-life and power project for large maps. |
| **Perchlorate Neutralization Grid** | Early-Mid | Perchlorate Remediation | Area-wide soil detoxification bonus. Speeds safe agriculture and biology. | Perchlorates are a real early barrier; solving them at scale matters. |

**Design goals:**  
- At least one strong early project (Atmospheric Accelerator) that rewards the terraforming focus.  
- Multiple mid-game options so different factions and strategies have something to race for.  
- Late projects clearly feed peace or transcendence without making other victories impossible.  
- Effects are powerful but not auto-win; counterplay via probes, military, or competing projects exists.
