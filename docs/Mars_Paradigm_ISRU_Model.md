# Mars Paradigm – Early-Game ISRU Loop & Resource Model
**Goal:** Positive feedback loops from limited starting mass. Educational focus on real constraints.

## Core Resources
- **Energy** (kWh equivalent) – Universal bottleneck. Solar (variable, dust-affected), nuclear (reliable, higher upfront).
- **Water** – From ice. Used for life support, agriculture, electrolysis (H₂ + O₂), Sabatier.
- **Oxygen** – Life support + oxidizer. Produced via electrolysis or MOXIE-style CO₂ processing.
- **Methane / Propellant** – Sabatier product. Enables mobility, energy storage, later launches.
- **Regolith Feedstock** – Processed soil → metals, silicon, construction material, aerosol precursors.
- **Organics / Biomass** – Later, from biology or biomanufacturing.
- **Research** – Abstract points from labs + energy.

**Starting package (per faction, slight variation):**  
Small fission or large solar array, one basic excavator/processor, one habitat, limited water and oxygen reserves, seed stock, basic former.

## Key Processes (Early)
1. **Power generation** → enables everything.
2. **Ice mining + purification** → Water.
3. **Electrolysis** → O₂ + H₂ (power-heavy).
4. **CO₂ capture + Sabatier** → CH₄ + additional O₂ (recycles H₂).
5. **Regolith processing** → Construction materials + metals.
6. **Habitat expansion & maintenance** – Consumes O₂, water, energy, materials.

## Positive Loop Example
Mine ice → electrolyze → produce O₂ and H₂ → Sabatier with atmospheric CO₂ → methane + more O₂ → use methane/oxygen for extended rover range or stored energy → mine more ice and regolith farther away → build more processors and power → repeat.

**Failure modes (visible and solvable):**
- Power shortage stops processing.
- Water depletion in local ice.
- Dust reduces solar.
- Perchlorate contamination slows biology/agriculture until remediated.
- Maintenance debt if industry outruns life support.

## Mid-game Transition
Once basic loops are stable, surplus energy/materials/propellant fund:
- More formers for terraforming.
- Larger processors.
- Research labs.
- Preparation for Paradigm projects.

**Abstraction level:** Rates are game-balanced but directionally accurate (power is king early; water location matters; propellant enables mobility). Tooltips explain the real chemistry (Sabatier: CO₂ + 4H₂ → CH₄ + 2H₂O; electrolysis, etc.).

This model directly supports the educational goal and feeds both economic strategies and terraforming expansion.
